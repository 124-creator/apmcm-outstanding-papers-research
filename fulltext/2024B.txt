2024APMCM summary sheet 
This problem pertains to optimization planning, where single-objective and multi-
objective programming models were established to determine the optimal shape and size 
parameters of the equipment using various suitable optimization algorithms. 
For Problem 1, a high-performance air-conditioner optimization model was developed, 
simulating temperature variations in summer and winter under different influencing factors, 
and designing the optimal shape and size. Initially, the Navier-Stokes equation was used to 
describe airflow. Subsequently, a single-objective programming model was established with 
objectives of temperature uniformity, energy efficiency, and noise control, with volume as a 
constraint. The model was solved using a combination of random search and finite difference 
methods, identifying wind speed, quantity, and spatial position as key factors, with the optimal 
shape and size being a cube of 0.5m. Simulations of temperature regulation in summer and 
winter showed better performance in summer. 
For Problem 2, a high-performance air purifier optimization model was established to 
design the optimal shape and size. The Advection-Diffusion equation was used to simulate 
molecular diffusion. A single-objective programming model was then established with 
maximum purification efficiency as the objective and purifier volume as the constraint, solved 
using the gradient descent algorithm. The optimal shape and size were determined as a 
rectangular prism of 0.82m×0.92m×1.18m. Robustness testing showed minimal impact on 
purification under uneven pollutant distribution, indicating high model robustness. 
For Problem 3, a high-performance humidifier optimization model was developed to 
determine the optimal shape and size. Fick's law was introduced to establish a water vapor 
diffusion equation. A single-objective programming model was then established with optimal 
humidification effect as the objective and volume limitation as the constraint, solved using the 
BFGS optimization algorithm. The optimal shape and size were found to be a rectangular prism 
of 1.26m×0.2m×0.5m. Sensitivity analysis showed minimal change in maximum 
humidification efficiency with increasing power, indicating good model stability. 
For Problem 4, a Pareto optimization model was established to determine the optimal 
shape and size of a three-in-one device. Building on the previous models, this model 
comprehensively considered energy efficiency, human comfort, purification effect, and 
humidification effect maximization as objectives, establishing a multi-objective programming 
model. The model was solved using the SLSQP algorithm, yielding an optimal shape and size 
for the three-in-one device as a rectangular prism of 1.24m×1.24m×2.15m, with defined height 
ranges for each functional module within the device. 
Keywords: Navier-Stokes equation; Adveaux-diffusion equation; Fick's law; various 
optimization algorithms

1 Introduction 
1.1 Problem Background 
At present, as economic development enters a high-quality stage, people's demand 
for quality of life is also increasing. As far as environmental electrical equipment is 
concerned, environmental conditioning devices combining air conditioners, 
humidifiers and air purifiers are gradually emerging. The device fully integrates the 
three devices and replenishes each other. The air conditioner will dry the indoor air 
while adjusting the temperature, and the humidifier can replenish moisture in time to 
properly adjust the indoor temperature. And when the indoor air circulation caused by 
the operation of the air conditioner, the air purifier can filter harmful pollutants in the 
air, purify the circulating air, and improve the indoor air quality. In addition, the three-
in-one device is equipped with electrical appliances that control 3 devices at the same 
time, reducing the power supply and connecting wires of multiple devices, and reducing 
the potential safety hazards of excessive lines.  
Through variable refrigerant flow, the air conditioner integrates the compressor, 
condenser and evaporator to form an artificial entity system with the function of 
providing a comfortable indoor environment. The working principle of the air purifier 
is to use high-voltage electricity to convert air molecules into positive ions and free 
electrons, so that they are adsorbed and deposited on the deposition plate. When the air 
enters the photocatalyst catalytic decomposition area, the harmful substances in the air 
are decomposed into negative oxygen ions and water. At this point, the air purification 
work is completed. The original reason of the humidifier is that alternating current is 
transformed into direct current through transformer step-down rectification, and then 
the direct current is transformed into high-frequency alternating current through an 
oscillation circuit and added to ultrasonic atomization. If external force is applied to it 
to deform, it will output corresponding voltage and current. On the contrary, applying 
alternating voltage to it will produce corresponding deformation, that is, vibration. In 
this way, the ceramic oscillator is driven by the high frequency voltage to produce 
ultrasonic oscillation, so that the water is atomized, and then the water mist is sent out 
by the fan to achieve the effect of humidifying the air.

2.1 analysis of problem 1 
This question requires the establishment of a model to simulate the change of 
indoor temperature with time and space in different seasons, establish an optimization 
model, and design the best shape and size of the air conditioner when the air conditioner 
reaches the maximum adjustment effect. Considering that the air flow generated by the 
air-conditioning gas in the flow process belongs to the compressible Newtonian fluid,

model. Then, the random search algorithm solves the above model, and determines the 
best shape and size of the air conditioner under the change of room temperature with 
time and space in summer and winter, as well as the best temperature regulation effect.

6.1.2 The establishment of high-performance air-conditioning optimization model. 
Question given the relevant factors will be on the air conditioning working state 
to make a certain degree of adjustment, thus affecting the air conditioning refrigeration 
effect, this part will be 6.1.1 section of the establishment of the flow temperature 
differential equation [2]based on, respectively, on the air conditioning design to discuss. 
The relevant factors given can be divided into five categories. The 1 category is the 
position of the air conditioner and the position of the air inlet. The different positions 
will determine the flow path of the air flow, thus affecting the uniformity of the 
temperature in a given room. The second category is the number of air inlets. The 
increase in the number of air inlets will improve the air circulation, but too many air 
inlets will also increase the difficulty of air conditioning design, so the advantages and 
disadvantages need to be considered in a balanced manner. The third category is the 
direction and angle of air inlets, it can determine the range that the air flow can cover, 
and a better angle can further improve the use effectiveness of the air conditioner. The 
fourth category is air volume, which determines the speed of air replacement, thus 
affecting the working efficiency of the air conditioner. The last category is wind speed, 
which should be moderate. If the wind speed is too high, it will lead to uncomfortable 
reaction of users, and noise will be generated when the wind speed is higher, which will 
affect the user's use feeling.

that makes the target function reach the minimum or maximum value is finally found.

difference method [4]to optimize the solution of the model on the basis of random search 
algorithm. The finite differential method is the numerical solution of the 1 differential

dispersion and space dispersion, and is widely used in the fields of computational fluid 
mechanics, computational heat transfer, computational electromagnetics and so on.

6.4.2 solution of the model 
In this paper, the SLSQP algorithm [11]is used to solve the above multi-objective 
planning model. Sequential least squares is the 1 numerical method to solve 
unconstrained nonlinear optimization problems[12]. Based on the idea of serialization, it 
decomposes the problem into a series of sub-problems to solve. In each subproblem, it 
is solved by minimizing a quadratic function.  
By using machine learning-SLSQP algorithm to solve the above multi-objective 
planning model, the best three-in-one equipment is determined. Its best size is air 
conditioner: width = 1.24m, depth = 1.24m, and height range of air conditioner: 
1.5~2.15m; Air purifier: width = 1.24m, depth = 1.24m, height range of humidifier: 
0.85~1.5m; Humidifier: width = 1.24m, depth = 1.24m, height range of air purifier: 
0~0.85m; According to this, the best appearance shape of the equipment can be drawn 
in this paper, as shown in the following figure:

7.1.2 Disadvantages of the model 
 The model assumes room air as incompressible ideal gas, neglecting practical 
factors like wall thermal capacity, which simplifies calculations but may lead to 
discrepancies with reality. It focuses on parameters like temperature, humidity, and air 
quality, with limited consideration for other environmental factors (e.g., lighting, wind 
speed variations) that could significantly impact device performance in real-world 
settings. To enhance model accuracy and practicability, more practical factors such as 
wall thermal capacity and indoor lighting changes can be incorporated. 
 Device size and shape designs presented in the article are based primarily on 
theoretical calculations and simulations, potentially limited by practical constraints like 
installation space and power supply. Furthermore, device durability and long-term 
operational stability require further validation. Advanced optimization algorithms (e.g., 
Particle Swarm Optimization) can be employed to address conflicts in multi-objective 
optimization, seeking better trade-off solutions. Additionally, more constraints can be 
introduced to ensure design feasibility in real-world environments.  
7.2 generalization of the model 
 The model in this paper can be applied to the smart home system to realize the 
automatic adjustment and management of indoor air environment by integrating the 
functional modules such as air conditioner, air purifier and humidifier. This will 
improve the user's living comfort and health level.  
 In the industrial environment, similar multi-objective optimization design can also 
be used for the design and optimization of the workshop air conditioning system to 
improve the comfort and safety of the working environment. For example, in high 
temperature, high humidity or polluted environments, the design of air conditioners, 
dehumidifiers and air purification equipment is optimized to ensure that the air quality 
in the work area meets the standard requirements.
