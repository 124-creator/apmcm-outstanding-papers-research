## 2. 题面逐页要点（短引与页码）

第 1 页图注原文：

> Figure 1. Schematic diagram of air conditioner, humidifier, and air purifier.

译文：图 1. 空调、加湿器与空气净化器示意图。  
图本身是落地空调、加湿器、净化器三件商品外观照片/示意，**没有尺寸标注，不是尺寸表或数据表**。题面已写明 *Schematic diagram*。

第 2 页硬约束原文：

> There is currently a room that is about to be renovated, which can be approximated as a long square with a spatial volume of 5m×8m×3m. The maximum volume of the limited three in one air conditioner is 0.1 m3, the rated power consumption is 1800W, the maximum air outlet speed is 8.0 m/s, the maximum inlet flow rate of the airflow is 600 m3/hour, and the maximum outlet flow rate of the airflow is also 600 m3/hour.

译文：现有一间即将装修的房间，可近似为长方体，空间体积为 5 m×8 m×3 m。三合一空调的最大体积限制为 0.1 m³，额定功耗为 1800 W，最大出风速度为 8.0 m/s，气流最大进口流量为 600 m³/h，最大出口流量同样为 600 m³/h。

第 2 页要求自行收集市场数据的原文（不是“附件已给出”）：

> As the manager of the APMCM three-in-one air conditioner appearance optimization team, please collect and research relevant data on air conditioners, humidifiers, and air purifiers in the market, and then establish a mathematical model for optimizing the appearance of the three in one air conditioner.

译文：作为 APMCM 三合一空调外观优化团队的负责人，请收集并研究市场上空调、加湿器和空气净化器的相关数据，然后建立三合一空调外观优化的数学模型。

全文检索（data / attachment / appendix / excel / csv / xls / download / table / http / dataset / provided 等）：除上述 *collect and research relevant data … in the market*、图 1 的 *Figure*、以及 *replenish moisture* 中与 data 无关的用词外，**没有指向官方数据表、附件或下载链接的句子**。第 3 页仅为第 4 问收尾与 *Glossary: Aerodynamics*，无数据表。
